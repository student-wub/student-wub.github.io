About

From drawings to the site

I am a civil engineer from Sirajganj, Bangladesh. Since completing my Diploma in Civil Technology in 2023, I have worked on an industrial building in Nilphamari and on piling projects at Mirsarai Economic Zone and the Pagla Sewage Treatment Plant in Dhaka.

On site I check that work follows the approved drawings and specifications, inspect materials, keep progress records and coordinate with contractors, supervisors and project engineers. In the office I prepare BOQs, verify quantities in Excel and draft in AutoCAD.

I am now studying for a B.Sc. in Civil Engineering at World University of Bangladesh and looking for my next site engineering role.
